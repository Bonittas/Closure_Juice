01 Picked at dawn

Strawberries,
straight from the field.

Every Berrylane shake starts with a bowl of ripe, glossy strawberries, delivered each morning from small farms in the highlands and hand-sorted before we open.

02 Into the tumble

Ripe, red and
falling for you.

Straight from the bowl, every berry tumbles into its shake whole. Sun-warm, deep red to the shoulder and fragrant enough to smell across the counter. Nothing frozen, nothing from a carton.

03 Milk & blend

Berries, cold milk,
forty-five seconds.

The berries land in the jar and we pour ice-cold whole milk right over them, with a scoop of house vanilla gelato. Lid on, blades up, and in under a minute it all turns silky, blush-pink and impossibly thick.

  • MilkWhole, oat or almond
  • Sweetened withRaw honey
  • Add-insMalt, rose, black pepper

04 The pour

Thick enough
to take its time.

Straight from the blender, a proper shake doesn't splash, it ribbons. One long, slow, velvety pour, so every glass gets the same thick body from the first sip to the last scrape of the straw.

05 Into the glass

Whipped cream, syrup,
one perfect berry.

The ribbon fills a frosted glass to the brim. Then piped Chantilly cream, a drizzle of slow-cooked strawberry syrup, a whole berry on top and a paper straw. Cold enough to fog.

  • CreamHand-whipped Chantilly
  • SyrupStrawberry, simmered 3 h
  • StrawPaper, compostable
